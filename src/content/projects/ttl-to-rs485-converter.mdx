## Motivation

A utility converter board for interfacing 3.3 V/5 V TTL microcontroller UARTs with RS485 bus devices (Modbus instruments, industrial sensors). Designed in EasyEDA for direct PCB fabrication.

## Key Components

- **SN75HVD08D** — 3.3 V RS485 transceiver with integrated fail-safe
- **74HC04D** — hex inverter used for direction-control signal conditioning
- **AMS1117-3.3** — on-board 3.3 V regulator for powering the transceiver

## Challenges & How They Were Solved

**Undersized AMS1117 output capacitor** — The initial schematic used a 10 µF output cap on the AMS1117, which is below the minimum stable value for this regulator family under load transients. Caught in a design review; changed to 22 µF tantalum before sending to fab.

**Voltage drop from reverse-protection diode** — A series Schottky diode was added on the input power rail for reverse-polarity protection. Under 5 V supply, the 0.3 V diode forward drop left only 4.7 V reaching the regulator's input — marginal for reliable 3.3 V regulation. Replaced with a P-channel MOSFET ideal-diode circuit (zero effective drop) before fabrication.

## Outcome

Board fabricated and functional. Reliable bidirectional RS485 communication at up to 250 kbps. Used as a test utility for Modbus instrument commissioning.
