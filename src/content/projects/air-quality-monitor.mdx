## Overview

A standalone IoT air-quality monitor built around an ESP32-WROOM DevKit on a custom PCB. It measures airborne particulate matter and indoor environmental conditions, and shows the readings in two places: a 1.3-inch 128 × 64 OLED on the board and a web dashboard that the ESP32 serves over Wi-Fi from its own web server.

Particulate matter (PM1.0, PM2.5, PM10) comes from a Plantower PMS7003 laser particle counter. Temperature, humidity, an indoor air quality (IAQ) index, CO₂ equivalent and breath-VOC equivalent come from a Bosch BME680 processed by the Bosch BSEC library.

## Features

- PM1.0, PM2.5 and PM10 in µg/m³ from the PMS7003 over UART2 (GPIO 16/17) at 9600 baud, 8N1.
- Temperature, relative humidity, IAQ index (0 to 500), CO₂ equivalent and breath-VOC equivalent from the BME680 via BSEC at I²C address 0x77.
- On-board OLED that switches between two pages every 5 seconds.
- Live web dashboard at the device IP, plus a JSON REST endpoint at /api/readings with CORS enabled.
- IAQ status levels: Good, Moderate, Poor, Unhealthy, Very Unhealthy and Hazardous.
- Dashboard warnings with editable thresholds, saved in the browser's local storage.
- Readings logged to the serial port at 115200 baud every 3 seconds; the on-board LED on GPIO 2 blinks if the sensor reports an error.
