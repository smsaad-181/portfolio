## Overview

A compact, self-contained wearable activity tracker on a single custom PCB. It tracks steps, cadence, distance, calories, active time and orientation in real time, shows them on a 0.96-inch OLED, and streams them to a Wi-Fi dashboard that any browser on the same network can open.

The board carries its own battery connector, USB-C charging and a one-button interface, so it needs no external modules or breadboard.

## Features

- Step counting from the BNO085's built-in hardware pedometer.
- Automatic activity classification into STILL, MOVE, WALK and RUN, using cadence thresholds with hysteresis plus linear-acceleration and gyro thresholds for MOVE.
- EMA-smoothed cadence in steps per minute; distance from per-step length (0.72 m walking, 0.90 m running).
- Calorie estimate from total steps, cumulative active time and peak linear acceleration in g.
- Roll, pitch and yaw from the BNO085 rotation vector, gyro magnitude in degrees per second, and live accuracy ratings (0–3) for each sensor report.
- OLED UI with 4 pages refreshed every 120 ms.
- One-button control: a short press moves to the next page, a press of 1.2 s or longer resets all stats; 40 ms debounce.
- Wi-Fi dashboard served by the ESP32, with live charts that poll a JSON endpoint every 800 ms.
- USB-C for power and firmware flashing, plus onboard Li-ion charging.
