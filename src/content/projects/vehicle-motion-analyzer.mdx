## Objective

A self-contained IoT motion analyzer that sits inside a vehicle and continuously monitors driving dynamics. It detects harsh driving events in real time, shows live data on an onboard OLED and serves a web dashboard over Wi-Fi, so any phone on the same network can monitor the drive or download trip logs.

Everything runs on one custom PCB: the ESP32-S3, an IMU, a magnetometer, the display, a Li-ion charger and a 3.3 V buck-boost supply. The dashboard is served by the ESP32-S3 itself, so no app or internet connection is needed.

## Key Components

| Component | Ref | Role |
|---|---|---|
| ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8 | U2 | Microcontroller with Wi-Fi and 8 MB flash; runs the firmware and web dashboard |
| MPU-6050 | U4 | Accelerometer and gyroscope for forward and lateral acceleration and yaw rate (I²C 0x68) |
| LIS3MDLTR | U5 | 3-axis magnetometer for the compass heading (I²C 0x1E) |
| DM-OLED096-636 | U1 | 0.96″ SSD1306 OLED module (I²C 0x3C) |
| CP2104-F03-GMR | U3 | USB-to-UART bridge that programs and monitors the ESP32-S3 over USB-C |
| MMBT2222A | Q1, Q2 | DTR/RTS auto-programmer driving GPIO0 and EN |
| BQ24092DGQR | U6 | Single-cell Li-ion charger, up to 500 mA from USB |
| 0603L100SLYR | U7 | Resettable PTC fuse in the USB input to the charger |
| TPS63020DSJT | U10 | Buck-boost converter that makes the 3.3 V rail from the battery |
| XFL4020-152MEC | L2 | 1.5 µH buck-boost inductor |
| TYPE-C-31-M-12 | USBC1 | USB-C connector for charging and programming |
| SK12D07VG3 | SW1 | Main power switch between the battery and the 3.3 V converter |
| XH-2A | CN1 | 1-cell Li-ion / Li-Po battery connector |
| Passives | R1–R20, C1–C31 | Pull-ups, battery divider, charger setting, decoupling and filtering |

## Features

- Harsh acceleration, harsh braking and sharp left/right turn detection with configurable thresholds (default 2.2 m/s², 2.8 m/s² and 25 °/s or 2.0 m/s² lateral, each sustained for 450 ms, with a 1200 ms gap between events).
- Tilt-compensated compass heading with declination correction and circular smoothing.
- Live OLED HUD showing heading, active event, forward and lateral acceleration, yaw rate and event counters.
- Wi-Fi web dashboard served from the ESP32-S3, refreshing from /data five times a second, with a colour-coded event banner, G-force circle, compass dial, live readings and day/night theme.
- Trip logging to a CSV file in LittleFS at 10 rows per second, with start, stop, clear and download from the dashboard.
- Accelerometer and gyroscope bias calibration at boot, with magnetometer offset and scale calibration loaded from non-volatile storage.
- Shared I²C bus at 400 kHz on GPIO8 and GPIO9 for the IMU, magnetometer and OLED.
- Battery voltage divider (100 kΩ / 36 kΩ) to GPIO1, and sensor interrupt lines to GPIO12 and GPIO13, wired for future firmware use.

## Hardware Design

### Power

USB-C feeds the BQ24092 charger through a resettable PTC fuse. ISET2 is tied high to cap the charge current at 500 mA, and two LEDs show power-good and charging. SW1 switches the battery to the TPS63020 buck-boost converter, which makes the 3.3 V rail.

### Controller

The ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8 sits at the top-left of the board with its antenna over a copper-free cut-out. A CP2104 bridges USB-C to UART0, and two MMBT2222A transistors driven by DTR and RTS put the module into download mode or reset it.

### Sensors

The MPU6050 and LIS3MDL share the I²C bus with the OLED at addresses 0x68, 0x1E and 0x3C. The MPU6050 sits in the lower-left corner and the LIS3MDL just below the ESP32-S3 module.

### PCB

A two-layer 59.4 × 49.9 mm board with ground pours on both sides and four grounded M3 mounting holes. The OLED module mounts above the board on the right.

## Outcome

- Full fabrication package in the project folder: schematic PDF, EasyEDA Pro project, Gerbers, BOM, pick-and-place file and STEP model.
- Arduino firmware (main.ino) with the event detector, compass, OLED HUD, web server and CSV logging, plus the embedded dashboard page in webpage.h.
- A build article published on GitHub Pages explains every circuit block with schematic crops, simulations of the charger, buck-boost, battery divider, auto-reset, event detector and compass, sensor viewers, an interactive 3D board model and firmware workflow drawings.
- Documented issues for the next revision: MPU6050 VDD has no 3V3 connection, TPS63020 VINA, EN and PS/SYNC are not joined to VBAT-OUT, C11 is 220 pF where the datasheet specifies 2.2 nF, Q2 has collector and emitter swapped with no BOOT/RESET fallback, and the BQ24092 has no power path.
- The battery voltage and the sensor interrupt pins are wired but not read by the current firmware.
