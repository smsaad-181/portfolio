## Overview

An ESP32-based patient vitals monitor that reads an ECG waveform and heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO₂), body temperature and room conditions, and presents them on a live web dashboard. The dashboard is served straight from the ESP32 over Wi-Fi, so no cloud service, phone app or external server is involved.

The hardware is a custom through-hole carrier PCB that brings together the ESP32 DevKit, an ECG front end, a pulse oximeter, a 16-bit ADC and an environmental sensor. It is an educational prototype, not a certified medical device.

## Features

- ECG waveform from the AD8232, digitized by the ADS1115 on A0, shown as a live scrolling trace with R-peak heart rate and lead-off detection.
- SpO₂ percentage with finger detection from the MAX30102.
- Body temperature from a 10 kΩ NTC divider on ADS1115 A1, converted with the Steinhart beta equation (B = 3950).
- Room temperature and relative humidity from the BME280.
- One shared I²C bus on GPIO 21/22 for the ADS1115 (0x48), BME280 (0x76) and MAX30102 (0x57); the ADS1115 runs at gain ×1 and 128 SPS.
- Web server on port 80; the browser polls two JSON endpoints and draws everything client-side.
- Patient record kept in browser storage: name, ID, bed, ward, age, blood group, gender, contact and notes.
- Data logging at a 2 s to 60 s interval, up to 500 rows, with Excel (.xls) export, plus rule-based alerts with suggestions.
